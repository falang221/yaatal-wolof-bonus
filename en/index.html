5-Minute Daily Family Ritual

  • Morning: Ask "Naka suba si?" at breakfast and practice "Jërëjëf / Ñoo ko bokk" at the table.
  • With family: Point and say "Sama..." (Sama yaay, Sama baay, Sama mag).
  • On stairs: Count steps together from Benn (1) to Fukk (10).
